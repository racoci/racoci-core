# Holds / RACOCI Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice and Tone
The voice is highly technical, authoritative, yet clean and minimalist.
- Avoid unnecessary fluff or decoration.
- Emphasize precision, mathematical models (DPO rewrites, category theory), and performance efficiency (Wasm memory, frame rate).

## UI/UX Design Principles
- **Futuristic & Cybernetic Theme:** Dominated by deep space backgrounds (`#0b0f19`), bright cyans (`#66fcf1`), vibrant purples (`#a855f7`), and bright greens (`#22c55e`).
- **Flexible Tiling:** Inspired by Blender's interface, allowing fully customizable window layouts, splitting, resizing, and dragging headers to reposition widgets.
- **Visual Feedback:** Smooth animations, glow effects, and state indicators.
- **Upright Text:** Labels and texts should always be oriented correctly relative to the user (e.g. 180-degree flip corrections for rotated text).
